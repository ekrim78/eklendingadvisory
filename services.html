Services

Engagements

Embedded lending programs can be complex to evaluate, intensive to launch, and painful to unwind if they fail. I've personally launched and overseen dozens of embedded capital programs and seen first-hand what works and what doesn't.

Partner Selection & Program Strategy

Selecting the wrong lending partner can mean years locked into a program that underperforms — and when something goes wrong with a white-labeled product, it's your brand and your merchants that bear the cost.

~4 weeks
Details ↓
Scope

For platforms evaluating embedded lending for the first time. Covers the full partner selection process — from initial evaluation criteria through provider diligence and final recommendation — with structured guidance on what good looks like at every stage.

Deliverables
  • Partner evaluation framework & weighted scorecard
  • RFP review & advisory (up to 2 RFP rounds)
  • MSA review — commercial terms, operational obligations, performance incentives
  • Written go/no-go recommendation with rationale
  • Kickoff readiness brief to set up a strong launch
Launch Execution

Launches that slip or go live without proper infrastructure generate poor early origination — and an under-scoped integration can require significant active, manual management that pulls internal resources for months.

4–12 weeks
Details ↓
Scope

For platforms that have selected a provider and need to get to a strong go-live. I join your launch process as an embedded advisor — helping unblock decisions, pressure-test the plan, and make sure nothing critical gets missed in the push to launch.

Deliverables
  • Launch project plan with milestones, owners, and dependencies
  • Weekly launch call participation (up to 10 calls)
  • Pre-launch readiness review against a proven launch checklist
  • Go-live communication review (merchant-facing messaging & positioning)
  • 30-day post-launch check-in on early origination & issues
Program Audit

Underperforming programs rarely self-correct — and platforms often can't tell whether the root cause is the partner, the product, or the distribution without someone who's seen what good looks like from the inside.

2–4 weeks
Details ↓
Scope

For platforms with an existing program that is underperforming, stalled, or approaching a contract renewal. Structured review of origination performance, ops, and distribution — delivered as a written assessment with prioritized recommendations.

Deliverables
  • Performance data review & diagnostics (provided by platform)
  • Up to 3 stakeholder interviews (internal team members)
  • Written audit report with root cause analysis
  • Prioritized recommendations with 30/60/90-day action plan
Ongoing Advisory Retainer

Embedded lending decisions compound quickly — a poorly structured renewal, a missed origination lever, or a bad BD hire can set a program back by a year. Retainer clients get a standing sounding board before decisions are made, not after.

Details ↓
Scope

Ongoing access for platforms that want a senior advisor available as questions arise — partner negotiations, team hiring, program growth decisions, contract renewals.

What's included

  • Review of program performance and data, new integration initiatives, renewals, etc.
